Frequently asked questions

What everyone asks

See the frequently asked questions9 questions · free plan, tags, privacy and more See questions
Is it really free? What's the catch?

Yes. The ID, the QR code, the reminders, the scan alerts, the protected alert and the printable ID are free and will stay free. We do not ask for a card. The only paid items are the tags and the PVC ID card, and they are completely optional.

Do I need a password?

No, and we will never ask you for one. To sign in you type your email and receive a code; you enter it and that's it. That way nobody else can change your pet's ID or turn off lost mode, and you have nothing to remember.

Are the digital ID and the physical ID card the same thing?

No. The digital ID is free and you use it from your phone. From "Mis carnets" (My pet IDs) you can also download their foldable ID for free and print it at home. The PVC ID card is an optional product at $59.900 COP for those who want something sturdier.

Do I have to buy the tag?

No. The digital ID works without buying a tag. The tag is optional and keeps the Kolita Code and QR code on the collar at all times, in a sturdy format. It's up to you.

How do I order the tag and how do I pay?

First create your pet's ID, which is free. When you finish you receive their Kolita Code. Then tap "Order here" on the tag you like and fill in the form (in Spanish). We message you on WhatsApp with the shipping cost (paid separately) and the payment details.

Do I have to download an app?

No. Kolita works in the browser of any phone. Neither you nor the person who finds your pet needs to install anything — scanning the QR code with the camera is enough.

Who can see my details?

The ID shows your pet's details and your name. Your phone number is not shown, unless you turn on the option to show it while your pet is lost. If someone finds your pet, they first have to say who they are and where they saw them; that alert reaches your email and you decide whether to reply. Your email is never shown to anyone. All the details are in our Privacy Policy (in Spanish).

Does it work for cats and other pets?

Of course! Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds and any companion at home. Cats that like to explore are, in fact, the ones that need it most.

What happens if my pet gets lost?

You can turn on lost mode and their ID shows an alert. Whoever finds your pet scans the QR code, sees the ID and fills in a short alert: who they are, their WhatsApp, where they saw your pet and a photo. That alert reaches your email with the approximate location, and you decide whether to contact them. Your number is never exposed.
